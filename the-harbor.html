Community Dedication

Guardians of the Coast: Keeping Our Communities Strong

True transformation is never a solo voyage, and the sponsors anchored here in The Harbor are recognized for far more than what they give to us. These businesses, leaders, and neighbors are standard-bearers of community stewardship, consistently pouring their time, hearts, and resources into keeping our regions vibrant, resilient, and safe for everyone. By stepping up to build this mobile hygiene and restoration fleet, they aren't just funding an organization; they are actively investing in the preservation and wellness of our shared home. Through their commitment, they ensure that as we transition participants into stable lifestyles, we naturally prevent upstream contaminants from reaching our protected marine sanctuary—safeguarding native wildlife, vital channels, and our bay right from the source.
