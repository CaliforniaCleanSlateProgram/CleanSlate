The Harbor

A safe, stabilizing anchor point powered by the community pillars, businesses, and visionaries backing our fleet.

A vessel cannot weather a storm without a secure harbor. The individuals and businesses recognized below represent the foundations of our mission. Through their capital investments and workforce alignments, they provide the safe anchorage necessary to clear the path from survival to absolute stability for our unhoused neighbors.

Guardians of the Coast: Keeping Santa Cruz Strong

Santa Cruz is more than just a coordinate on a map—it is our home, our passion, and a beautiful community that we are deeply proud to protect. True community transformation is never a solo voyage, and the sponsors anchored here in The Harbor are recognized for far more than what they give to us. These local businesses, leaders, and neighbors are the standard-bearers of Santa Cruz hospitality, consistently pouring their time, hearts, and resources into countless initiatives that keep our county vibrant, resilient, and safe for everyone. By stepping up to build this mobile hygiene and restoration fleet, they aren't just funding an organization; they are actively investing in the preservation and wellness of the home we all love.
