The Shared Sanctuary Crisis

Protecting the Lifeblood of Our Coast

The Monterey Bay National Marine Sanctuary, our local coastal wildlife, and the pristine beauty of our shores are the absolute lifeblood of our regional waterfront economy. Visitors travel from around the world to experience an untouched marine ecosystem, which our tourism corridors and beachfront businesses rely on. Maintaining that pristine coastal experience is essential for our businesses to thrive.

The environmental challenges facing our immediate beach and tourist corridors are entirely preventable—if we act now to intercept them upstream to prevent further degradation while summer traffic is at its absolute peak.

The Root Problem: Unmanaged Upstream Waste
The Big Picture Strategy

Ocean Currents Do Not Respect City or County Lines

What happens on one side of the bay directly impacts the other. The California Clean Slate Program (CCSP) sees the bigger picture: by actively targeting and cleaning up the Santa Cruz watershed and its major tributaries, we are directly preventing massive amounts of pollution from migrating across the bay into neighboring waterfronts, including Cannery Row.

Though our operational reach has not yet expanded physically into Monterey County, our ultimate goal is to scale our mobile fleets to serve the entire Monterey Bay perimeter. Protecting Santa Cruz's tributaries directly preserves the collective sanctuary waters we all share.

Our Dual-Impact Solution Model

Through our custom heavy-duty mobile hygiene units, we provide critical upstream intervention and hot showers for individuals along local inland waterways. By establishing an intentional bridge toward stability, our program aims to systematically mitigate and draw people out of the messy, unmanaged encampments that accumulate upstream. We reduce waste, plastics, and debris at the source right now through active field deployments, working to prevent further runoff from migrating down to pollute our marine sanctuary, harm coastline wildlife, or litter beachfront business storefronts.
