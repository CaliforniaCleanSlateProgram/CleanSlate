The Heart Behind the Mission

Hello, my name is Mandel, Executive Director of the California Clean Slate Program. My life has taken me through some very different worlds, which is exactly how I’ve ended up here. I’ve patrolled the streets as a Deputy Sheriff, I've worked as a Substance Abuse Counselor in treatment facilities, and I have even experienced what it's like to not have a place called home. Through those distinct lenses, I’ve seen our community's challenges from every possible angle. I’ve seen the raw daily struggles of our unhoused neighbors, and I’ve listened to local business owners who arrive at their storefronts to face the complex realities of encampments right outside their doors.

"I bring more than just professional credentials to the table; I bring the perspective of someone who has personally walked the road to recovery. That experience is why I am entirely focused on creating a real, long-term continuum of care—because I know what it truly takes to reclaim your life."

When I’m not working to scale this program, you’ll most often find me out in the elements. I have a profound love for Santa Cruz, from the mountains to the sea. Whether I’m dirt bike riding through backcountry trails, fishing, scuba diving, or tracking our local wildlife, I feel most connected to this community when I’m completely immersed in its natural beauty. I thrive on genuine diversity—I love meeting new community members, exploring the arts, and finding creative, operational solutions to help those in crisis. I value those moments of connection, whether it’s a quiet conversation or coordinating with others who share a relentless passion for this unique corner of California.

That’s why this program is a crucial, missing element of our community—it is rooted in direct, human-to-human accountability. Our goal is to systematically dismantle the poverty loop by looking at the whole person. We work to help individuals rebuild their internal self-worth and confidence, connect them directly with professional recovery support, secure stable housing, and ultimately, bridge them into steady, gainful employment right here in our county.

I’m proud to be doing this work to make Santa Cruz a stronger, safer, and better place for everyone who lives, works, and builds a life here.
