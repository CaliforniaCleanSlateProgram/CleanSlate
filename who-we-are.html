The Heart Behind the Mission

Hi, I’m Mandel. I started the California Clean Slate Program because I love this community deeply, and it’s truly unfortunate to see us facing the challenges we are today. I really believe that if we approach these issues with the right mix of compassion and innovation, we can make a meaningful difference for everyone here.

My life has taken me through some very different worlds, which is how I’ve ended up here. I’ve patrolled as a Deputy Sheriff and worked as a Substance Abuse Counselor in residential treatment. Through those roles, I’ve seen the homelessness crisis from every possible angle.

I bring more than just professional credentials to the table; I bring the perspective of someone who has personally walked the road to alcohol recovery. That experience is why I am so focused on creating a real, long-term continuum of care—because I know what it truly takes to get back on one's feet.

When I’m not working to build this program, you’ll most often find me out in the elements. I have a profound love for Santa Cruz, from the mountains to the sea. Whether I’m dirtbike riding through the trails, fishing, scuba diving, or just taking in the local wildlife on land and sea, I feel most connected to this community when I’m immersed in its natural beauty. I value those moments of connection, whether it’s a quiet afternoon or socializing with friends who share a passion for this unique corner of California.

That’s why I created this program—it’s based on real, human connection. My goal is to tackle the "poverty loop" by looking at the whole person. We work to help individuals rebuild their self-worth and confidence, connect them with the right recovery support, secure stable housing, and ultimately, find steady, gainful employment right here in our county.
