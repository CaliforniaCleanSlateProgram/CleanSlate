The Heart Behind the Mission

My life has taken me through some very different worlds, which is how I’ve ended up here. I’ve patrolled the streets as a Deputy Sheriff, I've worked as a Substance Abuse Counselor in treatment facilities, and even experienced what it's like to not have a place called home. Through those roles, I’ve seen the homelessness crisis from every possible angle. I’ve seen the daily struggles of our unhoused neighbors, and I’ve listened to business owners who are rightfully frustrated when they arrive at their storefronts to find encampments in front of their doors.

I bring more than just professional credentials to the table; I bring the perspective of someone who has personally walked the road to alcohol recovery. That experience is why I am so focused and passionate on creating a real, long-term continuum of care—because I know what it truly takes to get back on one's feet.

When I’m not working to build this program, you’ll most often find me out in the elements. I have a profound love for Santa Cruz, from the mountains to the sea. Whether I’m dirt bike riding through trails, fishing, scuba diving, or just taking in the local wildlife, I feel most connected to this community when I’m immersed in its natural beauty. I’m a person who thrives on diversity—I love meeting new community members, exploring the arts, and finding creative ways to help those in need. I value those moments of connection, whether it’s a quiet afternoon or socializing with others who share a passion for this unique corner of California.

That’s why this program is a crucial missing element of our community—it’s based on real, human connection. My goal is to tackle the "poverty loop" by looking at the whole person. We work to help individuals rebuild their self-worth and confidence, connect them with the right recovery support, secure stable housing, and ultimately, find steady, gainful employment right here in our county.

I’m proud to be doing this work to make Santa Cruz a better place for everyone who lives and works here.
