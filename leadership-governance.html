Leadership & Governance

Combining frontline operational capability with strict fiscal accountability to build lasting community infrastructure.

Mandel Coulter

Founder & Executive Director

Mandel Coulter brings a unique combination of tactical leadership, disciplined execution, and profound community empathy to the California Clean Slate Program. A former Deputy Sheriff and Coroner III, Mandel spent years operating within complex, high-stakes public safety systems. Transitioning his commitment to service into systemic community advocacy, he later served as a Substance Abuse Counselor within local treatment facilities and pursued specialized behavioral science and counseling studies at Modesto Junior College. This rare continuum of experience—from public safety systems to frontline behavioral health—drives his passion for engineering the critical path from crisis to self-sufficiency.

"I bring more than just professional credentials to the table; I bring the perspective of someone who has personally navigated the road to recovery. That experience is why I am entirely focused on creating a transparent, accountability-driven continuum of care—because I know exactly what it takes to reclaim a life."

Through these distinct professional lenses, Mandel has analyzed the homelessness and addiction crisis from every angle—balancing the raw, human needs of unhoused individuals with the structural security required by local business communities. As Executive Director, he oversees strategic vision, field deployments, and operational partnerships, engineering long-term programs that systematically restore personal self-worth, rebuild families, and foster long-term community wellness.

Board of Directors

Fiduciary Transparency & Compliance

The California Clean Slate Program is a registered California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation officially incorporated on May 8, 2026. Our independent Board of Directors operates under formal corporate bylaws and a strict Conflict of Interest Policy to maintain total fiscal integrity and operational accountability. For institutional due diligence vetting, foundational inquiries, or copies of regulatory filings, please contact our administrative offices directly.
