Host a Mobile Service Unit

Our "Streets to Sheets" mission relies on strategic access. We are looking for property owners, churches, community centers, and businesses willing to open their space for our Mobile Service Units. Community Retail Partnerships

We believe in the power of local impact. Just as local markets empower shoppers to support charities through small, daily actions, we are seeking retail partners who want to integrate the California Clean Slate Program into their customer experience. Whether through donation-at-register programs, supply drives, or community awareness initiatives, we want to build a bridge between your customers and the neighbors who need a fresh start.

Ready to join the movement?

Email our team at partnerships@californiacleanslateprogram.org to start a conversation about hosting a unit or becoming a retail partner.
