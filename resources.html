Community Resource Hub

A curated directory of trusted crisis lines, basic needs support, and regional service networks across Santa Cruz County.

Immediate Crisis Support

If you are experiencing an immediate medical emergency, call 911. For 24/7 mental health crisis support, call or text 988.

1. Immediate Crisis & Emergency Support

24/7 hotlines and crisis intervention clearinghouses.

211 Santa Cruz County

The central clearinghouse for local health, human services, housing, and emergency support networks.

Access: Dial 2-1-1 or text your ZIP code to 211

County Mobile Crisis Response

24/7 confidential professional support for mental health emergencies and urgent field assessments.

Access: Call 1-800-952-2335 or dial 988

2. Basic Needs, Food & Health

Essential nutritional distribution and clinical care access.

Second Harvest Food Bank

Primary community food distribution network and emergency food pantries throughout the region.

Access: Call (831) 662-0991

Homeless Persons' Health Project (HPHP)

Comprehensive medical care, clinical support, and health navigation services.

Access: Coral Street Campus, Santa Cruz

3. Housing Navigation & Shelter

Emergency shelter campuses and stabilization pathways.

Housing Matters

Comprehensive local service campus offering emergency shelter, case management, and pathways to permanent housing.

Access: Coral Street Facility, Santa Cruz

4. Legal & System Navigation

Regional legal aid and low-income community support.

Watsonville Law Center

Regional legal aid supporting low-income community members, worker rights, and clean slate record remedies.

Access: watsonvillelawcenter.org
