Frontline Fellowship

Mobilizing Regional Veterans to Serve Santa Cruz Communities through Low-Barrier, Stipend-Supported Field Service Opportunities.

The California Clean Slate Program is committed to supporting our community's veterans by breaking down financial survival hurdles through immediate, flexible program enrolment. We recognize the operational excellence, dedication, and leadership values that members of the regional veteran community bring to field execution. The **Frontline Fellowship** is structured to put local veterans at the helm of our mobile hygiene logistics, community stabilization paths, and environmental watershed preservation runs.

Stipend-Supported Field Service Days

Immediate, Low-Barrier Program Enrolment • Santa Cruz Base

Are you a local veteran looking for flexible, community-focused field opportunities that offer immediate, same-day financial support? We cut through traditional, rigid corporate delays to get you out serving the community immediately.

Traditional multi-week payroll setups, intensive screening cycles, and systemic banking requirements create severe obstacles for unhoused or transitioning veterans on the hunt for immediate support. Our fellowship operates an agile, peer-reviewed model. This initiative is explicitly designed for a fluid environment where you can step up to manage mobile hygiene trailers, run street-level distribution loops, and secure critical relief the same day your assignment concludes.

Immediate Same-Day Fellowship Stipends Discretionary subsistence funding delivered directly upon completion of your voluntary field service assignment to combat survival instability.
Fluid, Flexible Service Blocks Choose your deployment sessions around school schedules, family priorities, treatment programs, or existing daily routines.
Rapid, Low-Barrier Enrolment Pathway A straightforward, peer-to-peer sign-up model built for veterans. Get integrated into active community crews without red tape.
