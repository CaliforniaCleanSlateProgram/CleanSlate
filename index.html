A Note from the Founder

As a Substance Abuse Counselor and resident of Santa Cruz, I know that hygiene is the foundation of recovery. With recent local service closures, our community faces an urgent crisis. The California Clean Slate Program is our agile, "next-gen" response—bringing essential care directly to the streets to bridge the gap toward stabilization and recovery.

Phase 2: Mobile Service Units

The core of our mission: Deploying our 3-stall mobile unit for safe, dignified access to hot showers and sanitation.
