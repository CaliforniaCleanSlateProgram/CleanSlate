Our 5-Phase "Streets to Sheets" Continuum

Phase 1: Street Outreach & Hygiene

Building trust where it matters most. Providing immediate, baseline support to our unhoused neighbors.

Phase 2: Mobile Service Units

Deploying our 3-stall mobile unit for safe, dignified access to hot showers and sanitation.

Phase 3: Stabilization & Recovery Referrals

Acting as an intentional, warm bridge to licensed external partners for treatment and detox.

Phase 4: Sober Living Environments

Transitioning individuals into supportive housing with peer accountability and structured routines.

Phase 5: Community Reincorporation

Partnering with local workforce development to restore independence and complete the journey to a clean slate.
