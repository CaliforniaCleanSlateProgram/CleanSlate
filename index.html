Our Guiding Core Slogan

"Street-Level Compassion. Life-Changing Direction."

California Clean Slate Program is a Santa Cruz nonprofit bringing mobile showers and hygiene services directly to unhoused neighbors, with a path toward recovery, stable housing, and steady work.

We are not affiliated with the court system and do not provide criminal record expungement, legal representation, legal advice, or record-clearing services.

Why We Exist

Built by the Community, For the Community

Recovery doesn't happen alone. It happens when neighbors, local businesses, and people in crisis are connected — often starting with something as simple as a hot shower and a clean set of clothes.

California Clean Slate Program is a mobile shower and hygiene service for unhoused people in Santa Cruz County.

We bring mobile shower and hygiene services directly to where unhoused people already are, meeting them with compassion and respect — no appointment, no paperwork required. Need immediate help or local county resources? Explore our verified Community Resource Directory.

From there, we help connect people to Substance Use Disorder (SUD) recovery support, sober living environments (SLEs), and job placement through our Frontline Fellowship — while also helping reduce encampment waste along riverbeds and keep trash, plastics, and contaminants out of the protected Monterey Bay Sanctuary.

A hot shower and clean clothes offer something simple but real: dignity, and enough breathing room to consider what comes next. When people feel seen and supported, taking that first step toward treatment and stability becomes possible.

Through our five-step continuum and the Frontline Fellowship, that first step can lead to lasting recovery, cleaner public spaces, and a healthier coastline across Santa Cruz.

We are not part of the court system. We don't provide legal counsel, record expungement, or any record-clearing services — our work is entirely mobile hygiene, outreach, and stabilization support.

San Lorenzo River • Santa Cruz
Also Good for the Bay
Helping People and Protecting the Watershed, in the Same Visit

Many of the people we serve are living along the San Lorenzo River corridor and nearby waterways here in Santa Cruz. Our upcoming field deployments are focused on reaching unhoused individuals in these areas directly.

By bringing mobile hygiene units to these sites, we build trust that helps move people into our 5-step "Streets to Sheets" continuum of care. This outreach also serves as an upstream intervention: providing hygiene and stability alternatives right where people are living helps reduce waste, plastics, and debris before they can wash downstream into the Santa Cruz Harbor and the protected Monterey Bay National Marine Sanctuary.

Jobs, Not Just Services
The Frontline Fellowship

True stability includes steady work. Through our Frontline Fellowship program, people who've been through our continuum can go on to help manage our vehicle fleet, hygiene equipment, and field logistics. It's a paycheck, a purpose, and a way to give back to the same streets that once needed them most.
