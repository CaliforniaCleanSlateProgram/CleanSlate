Our 5-Phase "Streets to Sheets" Continuum

Phase 1: Street Outreach & Hygiene

Building direct, trust-based relationships on the streets. Our team meets participants where they are, providing essential resources and human connection.

Phase 2: Mobile Service Units

Restoring fundamental dignity through customized mobile hygiene systems. We deploy specialized truck and trailer setups to high-need areas.

Phase 3: Stabilization & Recovery Referrals

Acting as an intentional, warm bridge to licensed external partners. We guide participants smoothly from initial contact into appropriate treatment.

Phase 4: Sober Living Environments

Transitioning individuals into supportive, structured housing. Our sober living environments champion peer accountability and communal safety.

Phase 5: Community Reincorporation

The ultimate goal achieved. Through strategic workforce development partners, participants are matched with sustainable employment.
