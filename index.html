Our 5-Phase "Streets to Sheets" Continuum

Phase 1: Street Outreach & Hygiene

Building direct, trust-based relationships on the streets. Our team meets participants where they are, providing essential resources and human connection to offer immediate, baseline support.

Phase 2: Mobile Service Units

Restoring fundamental dignity through customized mobile hygiene systems. We deploy specialized truck and trailer setups to high-need areas, providing safe access to warm showers, fresh clothing, and resource navigation.

Phase 3: Stabilization & Recovery Referrals

Acting as an intentional, warm bridge to licensed external partners. We guide participants smoothly from initial contact into appropriate treatment or detox facilities, standing by them as they reach their personal milestones.

Phase 4: Sober Living Environments

Transitioning individuals into supportive, structured housing. Our sober living environments champion peer accountability, communal safety covenants, and a healthy routine that helps participants build an enduring, stable foundation.

Phase 5: Community Reincorporation

The ultimate goal achieved. Through strategic workforce development partners, participants are matched with sustainable employment opportunities—restoring independence and completing their journey to a clean slate.
