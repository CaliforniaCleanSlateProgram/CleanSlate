California Clean Slate Program

Street-Level Compassion. Life-Changing Direction.

The California Clean Slate Program is the Next-Gen of community stabilization. We don't just provide services; we engineer the critical path from the streets to self-sufficiency. By deploying proven mobile hygiene units as our point of entry, we build the trust necessary to act as an intentional, warm bridge to recovery and, ultimately, to sustainable housing and professional stability. We provide the structure for those ready to move from survival to stability—funding the future of our participants through a self-sustaining model of dignity and professional growth.

🌊 Safeguarding Coastal Wildlife & Ecosystems

Human Restoration. Sanctuary Preservation.

At the California Clean Slate Program, we evaluate community health through a broader lens. Our upcoming field deployments will purposefully target unhoused individuals along the San Lorenzo River and adjacent waterways. By deploying our mobile hygiene units directly to these critical sectors, we establish the trusted, foundational rapport required to transition participants seamlessly into our 5-phase "Streets to Sheets" continuum of care.

This dual-impact model serves as a vital upstream intervention mechanism. By offering physical, structured alternatives to individuals living in sensitive areas, we create a measurable drop in accumulated waste, plastics, and debris before they reach the ocean. Correcting these challenges at the source ensures cleaner downstream outcomes—meaning that every milestone achieved by our participants simultaneously shields our local marine sanctuary, preserves coastal habitats, and fosters a healthier marine ecosystem right from the start.
