A Healthy Community, A Clean Environment

Our historic shores, local businesses, and community environments thrive when every resident lives with health, structure, and basic human dignity. The visible crisis unfolding in our public spaces impacts our community health, tourism, and shared public ecosystems. Turning a blind eye solves nothing; temporary patches fall short.

The California Clean Slate Program (CCSP) breaks the cycle. By delivering immediate, mobile structural relief directly to the streets, we establish the trusted baseline needed to guide individuals out of crisis and back into stable, productive civic life.

The "Streets to Sheets" Continuum of Care

Phase 1

Street-Level Engagement

We meet unhoused individuals exactly where they are—in public spaces and river pathways—utilizing active outreach to build trust, assess immediate needs, and introduce accountability structures.

Phase 2

The Mobile Shower & Hygiene Fleet

Through custom, self-sustained mobile service vehicles, we provide private showers, fresh laundry facilities, and clean clothing exchanges. Physical cleanliness restores personal pride and serves as the immediate turning point toward self-worth.

Phase 3

Detoxification & Recovery Referrals

Hygiene bridges the gap to health. Once stabilization is initiated, our team immediately manages direct warm-handoff referrals to local detoxification services and professional substance abuse treatment facilities to address root-cause trauma.

Phase 4

Steady Sober Living Environments (SLE)

Post-treatment, we connect individuals into stable, managed Sober Living Environments. Here, they build consistent routines, volunteer or work internally with the CCSP mobile network, and establish a track record of accountability.

Phase 5

Full Community Reincorporation

The ultimate goal is complete civic restoration. We work closely with local businesses, commercial partners, and legal clearance programs to return healthy, dignified, and reliable individuals into the local job market—strengthening our workforce and local economy.

Fuel the Continuum

Every donation directly funds supplies, mobile fuel, operations, and resource networking to move individuals from the streets back into community success.
