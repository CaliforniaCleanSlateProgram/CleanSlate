The Bedrock to Buildout Strategy

Founded and directed by Mandel Coulter, a certified Substance Use Disorder professional with an extensive background in public safety, high-stakes logistics, and legal compliance, CCSP approaches civic stabilization through a rigid military operational discipline. Every deployment vector, supply line, and participant transition is mapped out with command-level precision and accountability.

As a Black founder executing this framework from the ground up, Coulter designed the California Clean Slate Program to replace hyper-local temporary band-aids with high-integrity infrastructure. We are entirely focused on our Santa Cruz home base, assembling the essential tools, refining strict compliance workflows, and organizing local networks today so that our upcoming initial launch stands as an unshakeable proof-of-concept for the state and federal corridors.

Human Stabilization Architecture

The "Streets to Sheets" Continuum

We transition participants out of crisis via a highly structured, five-phase tactical continuum that trades temporary containment for true, permanent life correction.

PHASE 01

Point of Entry

Restoring foundational dignity via premium mobile hygiene loops, heavy-duty hot continuous showers, and uniform exchange.

PHASE 02

Intentional Stabilization

Direct, proactive peer outreach, continuous case navigation mapping, and physical health alignment vectors.

PHASE 03

Safe/Sober Transition

Acting as a compassionate, warm bridge into highly focused substance recovery structures and clean, sober living housing assets.

PHASE 04

Workforce Development

Immediate integration into localized workforce pipelines, customized trade skill training, and career autonomy paths.

PHASE 05

Self-Sufficiency

Successful placement into permanent independent housing, long-term employment stability, and complete personal autonomy.

Asset Infrastructure

The Fleet Blueprint: Assets Built for Endurance

We do not deploy makeshift vans or unstable temporary setups. Our program is currently designing and specifying robust, $60k+ heavy-duty mobile hygiene trailers and custom utility support units engineered specifically for maximum mechanical lifecycles and absolute environmental safety.

By implementing custom-fabricated steel frames, industrial continuous tankless water arrays, self-contained onboard utility vaults, and stringent graywater capture loops, our fleet satisfies strict urban and ecological discharge rules. We are configuring this physical blueprint right here in Santa Cruz to establish a turnkey, flawless asset that can be seamlessly manufactured and shipped to any municipality in the United States facing identical crises.

Dual-Impact Conservation

The Harbor & Our Bay: Upstream Resource Protection

At CCSP, we evaluate civic health and environmental conservation as one single equation. Our upcoming deployment strategy purposefully targets unhoused individuals along the critical **San Lorenzo River corridors and sensitive waterways of Santa Cruz**.

By bringing our mobile assets directly to these sensitive sectors, we build immediate, trusted rapport to move individuals smoothly into our five-phase continuum. This creates a massive, measurable upstream advantage: by providing human-centric alternatives right at the critical source corridors, we actively prevent waste, plastics, and debris from ever flowing downstream into the Santa Cruz Harbor and the protected **Monterey Bay National Marine Sanctuary**.

This unique framework makes CCSP heavily fundable by major ocean conservation portfolios, federal agency allocations, and private ESG capital pools that traditional social services can never touch.
