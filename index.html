Our 5-Phase "Streets to Sheets" Continuum

Phase 1: Street Outreach & Hygiene

Building direct, trust-based relationships. Our team meets participants where they are, providing essential resources and dignity.

Phase 2: Mobile Service Units

Restoring fundamental dignity through customized mobile hygiene systems. We deploy specialized setups to high-need areas.

Phase 3: Stabilization & Recovery

Acting as an intentional, warm bridge to licensed partners. We guide participants smoothly from initial contact into treatment.

Phase 4: Sober Living Environments

Transitioning individuals into supportive housing. Peer accountability and communal safety are our foundations.

Phase 5: Community Reincorporation

The ultimate goal: participants are matched with sustainable employment and long-term self-sufficiency.
