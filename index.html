"The California Clean Slate Program is the NuEra of social integration. We don't just provide services; we engineer the critical path from the streets to self-sufficiency. By deploying proven mobile hygiene units as our point of entry, we build the trust necessary to act as an intentional, warm bridge to recovery and, ultimately, to sustainable housing and career reincorporation. We provide the structure for those ready to move from survival to stability—funding the future of our participants through a self-sustaining model of dignity and professional growth."
